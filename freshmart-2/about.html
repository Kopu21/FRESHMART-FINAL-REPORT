Rooted in Organic Agriculture

Rebuilding the Connection Between Real Soil and Your Plate

FreshMart began with a simple observation: supermarket produce spends an average of 14 days sitting in cold-storage transit losing up to 60% of natural vitamins. We created a direct farm dispatch pipeline that delivers within hours of morning harvest.

🌱

100% Pesticide Free

Every grower in our network is certified organic, using natural composting, ladybug pest control, and nitrogen cover crops.

🤝

Fair Farmer Equity

We pay partner farmers 40% above industrial supermarket benchmark wholesale rates, keeping small regional agriculture thriving.

⚡

Zero Single-Use Plastic

Orders are delivered in sanitized, reusable breathable hemp and wooden crates that we swap and sterilize every week.
