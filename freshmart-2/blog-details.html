The 10-Minute Heirloom Tomato & Genovese Basil Salad

Culinary Director, FreshMart Test Kitchen

Step-by-Step Preparation

1. The Slice: Cut the heirloom tomatoes into thick 1/2-inch slices and bite-sized wedges. Never refrigerate fresh heirloom tomatoes prior to serving, as cold temperatures break down volatile aroma compounds.

2. The Salt Draw: Arrange the tomato slices in a single layer on a wide ceramic platter. Sprinkle with a generous pinch of flaky sea salt and allow them to rest for 5 minutes. The salt draws out the sweet tomato nectar which forms the natural dressing base.

3. The Assembly: Tear the fresh Genovese basil leaves by hand (avoid cutting with a knife to prevent bruising the delicate edges). Scatter evenly over the tomatoes, nestle fresh burrata in the center, and drizzle cold-pressed olive oil generously.
